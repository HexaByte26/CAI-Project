The Sequential Circuits

The "Brain" of Modern Electronics

Have you ever wondered how an elevator knows exactly which floors to stop at? The answer lies in Sequential Logic.

Unlike basic logic circuits that only react to what is happening right now

The Secret Ingredient: Memory

Unlike basic logic circuits, sequential circuits have something special. They don't just react; they remember. Here is how they decide what to do:

1. Current Inputs

They look at the data coming in right now.

2. Past States

They remember exactly what happened a moment ago.

3. Next Action

They combine both pieces of information to decide what to do next.

Introducing Counters

One of the most important jobs of a sequential circuit is counting. In digital electronics, counters track time or divide frequencies.
